About me

What pulls me into engineering is data and systems that run efficiently. I'm studying Computer Engineering at UC3M, and my path goes from the metal up: RISC-V assembly and Motorola 68000 microinstructions on one end, applied data engineering on the other. My projects span supercar telemetry analysis on Kaggle datasets and Sharpe Lab, the custom real-time stock market terminal I built to make investment analysis faster.

Away from computers: cars. The incurable kind of passion.
